# PRODUCT.md — Smart Growth Tracker

## 1. Product Purpose & Value Proposition
Smart Growth Tracker is a clinical-grade early childhood developmental screening and pediatric growth tracking web application (PWA). It empowers parents to monitor child physical growth trajectories (Height, Weight, BMI) according to World Health Organization (WHO) Child Growth Standards and evaluate early milestone achievements according to Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) developmental screening guidelines.

## 2. Target Audience & Users
- **Primary Users (Parents & Caregivers)**: Need a reassuring, intuitive, highly legible, and parent-friendly interface. Many access on smartphones (360px–420px viewports) while holding a child. Text must be easy to read with high contrast, and touch targets must be large and ergonomic ($\ge 44\text{px}$).
- **Secondary Users (Pediatricians & Healthcare Providers)**: Receive WhatsApp shared reports and doctor notes. They need clean, chronological, quantitative metrics (age in months, percentiles, domain completion ratios, and bilingual responses in English + Hindi).
- **Administrative Users**: System admins reviewing multi-tenant child registries and machine learning classifier diagnostics.

## 3. Core Operational Context & Modes
- **Mode: Operate (Pediatric Utility)**: The interface must feel clean, trustworthy, and serene. It should never feel flashy, playful to the point of distraction, or cluttered.
- **Tone**: Warm clinical, calm, credible, parent-friendly, authoritative (WHO/CDC aligned).
- **Critical Constraints**: Non-diagnostic framing. All statistical ML outputs are decision support, not medical diagnoses.
