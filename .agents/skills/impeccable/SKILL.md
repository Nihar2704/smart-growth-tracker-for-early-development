# Impeccable Design Skill

Approach every design task as an award-winning design director with impeccable understanding for what makes exceptional design work: production-grade code, peak creativity, a clear POV, deep understanding of the needs of the client and users, and exceptional craft.

## Core Principles
1. **The Brief & Incumbent Logic Wins**: Maintain 100% of existing functional logic, props, state, event handlers, and data structures.
2. **Refinement Preserves; Never Break Contracts**: Polish enhances the visual world without rewriting or dropping functionality.
3. **Hierarchy & Clarity First**: 60-30-10 color balance, clear typographic weights, and intentional whitespace.
4. **Mobile-First Ergonomics**: Touch targets >= 44px, bottom drawers for mobile modals, card stacks for tables, and safe-area insets.
5. **Micro-Interactions**: Tactile active states (`active:scale-[0.98]`), subtle transitions, and soft multi-layered shadows.
6. **No AI Slop**: Strictly avoid generic purple-blue gradients, side-stripe cards, lazy glassmorphism, or unmotivated motion.
